Classic Ludo Rules

  • 1. Leaving Base: Roll a 6 to move a token from your corner yard onto your colored starting square.
  • 2. Bonus Rolls: Rolling a 6, capturing an opponent's token, or reaching the center Home triangle grants an immediate bonus roll!
  • 3. Three 6s Limit: Rolling three 6s in a row forfeits your current turn and passes the dice.
  • 4. Capturing: Landing on a square occupied by a lone opponent token sends it back to its base yard.
  • 5. Safe Star Squares: Colored starting squares and star squares (★) protect all tokens on them from being captured.
  • 6. Winning: Tokens must enter the colored home column and roll the exact number needed to reach the center goal.
